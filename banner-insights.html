Banner Insights

Scaling display creative from ~50 to as many as 300 banners.

Creative volume only mattered if production remained fast, consistent and measurable.

Production scale + measurement

I created assets for responsive Google Ads, dynamic remarketing, Smart Shopping and Discovery in formats including 1200×628, 1200×1200, 120×600, 160×600, 300×250, 300×600, 336×280, 468×60 and 970×90. During intensive periods, output reached up to roughly 300 banners per month, versus up to about 50 before my involvement.

I also paid attention to assisted signals that are easy to overlook in Google Ads reporting—view-through conversions, cross-device conversions and all conversions. These metrics helped build a longer-term view of display effectiveness beyond immediate last-click results.

Display should not be judged by a single metric in isolation. The useful question is how creative contributes to the path to conversion across time, devices and touchpoints.
