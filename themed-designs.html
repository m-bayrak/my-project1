Campaign Design

Themed creative as a commerce system.

Seasonal concepts helped make the store feel distinctive while supporting sales, brand consistency and faster cross-channel campaign execution.

One campaign, many surfaces

We developed themed materials for New Year, Black Friday, Valentine’s Day, Women’s Day, Vyshyvanka Day, Galaxy anniversaries, Samsung Electronics anniversaries, free delivery and rapid delivery in Kyiv during lockdown periods.

A theme could extend into GDN, Google Discovery, Shopping, social, large-format in-store displays, Viber, push notifications, landing pages, promotion pages, product markers and desktop/mobile website banners. Discounted products were also given campaign-specific product visuals so the same creative idea could continue into Shopping feeds.
